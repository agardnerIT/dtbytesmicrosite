# OTel Supports 12+ Languages — Including Erlang and Swift

When developers think about OTel language support, they typically think Java, Python, Go, JS, and .NET.

But OTel has **first-class SDKs for 12+ languages**, including some you might not expect:

- **Erlang / Elixir** — great for telecom and messaging workloads
- **Swift** — for iOS and macOS native app observability
- **Rust**, **Ruby**, **PHP**, and more

There's also a brand-new **Kotlin Multiplatform SDK** announced in 2026 for cross-platform mobile apps.
