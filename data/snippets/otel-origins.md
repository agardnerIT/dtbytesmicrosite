# OTel Was Born From Two Competing Projects Merging

OpenTelemetry didn't start from scratch. It was created in 2019 by **merging OpenTracing and OpenCensus** — two competing observability frameworks that had split the ecosystem.

Rather than let the fragmentation continue, the **CNCF facilitated a merger**, and both projects were formally archived once OTel reached maturity.

> OTel is now a **CNCF graduated project**, signalling broad adoption and a stable governance model.
