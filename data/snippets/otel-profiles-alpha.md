# OTel Has a 5th Signal: Profiles (Now in Public Alpha!)

Most people know OpenTelemetry collects **Traces, Metrics, Logs, and Baggage** — but there's now a **fifth signal: Profiles**.

Continuous profiling data (CPU, memory, allocations) entered public alpha in 2026, meaning you can ship profiling data over OTLP alongside your other telemetry.

> **Why it matters:** Profiles complete the picture — you can now correlate a slow span directly to the exact lines of code burning CPU at that moment, all through a single OTLP pipeline.
